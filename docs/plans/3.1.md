## Decisions

- **One instrument set, injected.** No package-level collectors, so tests get a fresh
  registry and nothing depends on init order. Metrics are optional everywhere they are
  used: a nil set records nothing, which is what keeps unit tests from needing one.
- **Status is bucketed to its class.** Alerting cares about the 5xx rate, not 502 versus
  503; per-code labels would multiply series for no operational gain.
- **The route label is chi's pattern.** `/v1/jobs/{jobId}` stays one series where
  `/v1/jobs/<uuid>` would create one per job, and anything unmatched collapses to
  `unmatched` so a 404 flood cannot become a flood of label values.
- **The tenant label is length-checked.** `usage_chars_total` is per tenant by contract,
  and an unbounded label is the classic way a metrics backend falls over.
- **RTF is not recorded for cache hits.** A hit would report a flattering number that
  says nothing about the fleet's speed.
- **Buckets are chosen around the promises they police**: TTFA resolution is dense below
  300 ms (NFR-01), queue wait spans ADR-007's 2 s and 10 s budgets, RTF straddles 1.
- **Every alert names the requirement it defends**, so a firing alert points at a promise
  rather than a number someone once picked. Dashboard thresholds match the alert
  expressions, so a red panel and a firing alert mean the same thing.
- **Grafana allows anonymous viewing in compose only.** It saves a login while debugging;
  production puts Grafana behind the operator's SSO, which is why it is not a default.

## The metrics immediately earned their keep

The first traffic after wiring them showed `http_requests_total{route="/v1/synthesize",
status="4xx"}` rather than the 2xx I expected. The cause was the voice catalogue gap
recorded as "known" in `docs/plans/1.9-1.13.md`: the first sync runs before any worker is
ready, so for up to 60 seconds the catalogue was empty and **every synthesis request
returned 422 unknown_voice**.

Recorded as a cosmetic startup nuisance, it was in fact a minute of total failure after
every deploy. The sync now polls every 2 seconds until the catalogue is populated and
then settles to the slow cadence: measured cold start is **0 s**, down from up to 60.
