# Task 3.4 — Runbook drills

Covers: NFR-04, NFR-12, T-20 (and T-105, T-106, T-107 added here).
Depends on: 3.3.

## Intent

Execute every procedure in `13-runbook.md` against a live stack. A runbook that has only
been reviewed is a hypothesis; the point of this task is to find out which parts are
false while nobody is paged.

Scope is deliberately "run it and fix what breaks", so the steps below are the drills, and
the fixes are whatever they surface. They surfaced four defects; each is recorded in
`docs/reports/drill-m3.md` with its evidence.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Automating the restore drill. It is quarterly and manual by design; the note stays in
  `10-tests.md` § gaps.
- A chaos step that restarts Redis inside the integration suite. It would have caught
  finding 1 a milestone earlier and is worth doing, but it is a test-infrastructure task,
  not this one. Recorded as gap 5.
- Drilling the model upgrade, which needs a second pinned revision to mean anything.
- Concurrent consumption of the job streams. The serial consumer is correct now that
  finding 5 is fixed; making it parallel is a throughput change touching the segment
  claim, the reconciler and ADR-003, and belongs in its own task with an ADR. Recorded in
  `10-tests.md` § gaps.
